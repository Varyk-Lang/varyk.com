weight = 70
+++

If you find a security vulnerability in the Varyk compiler, its generated code, or this website, please report it to [security@varyk.com](mailto:security@varyk.com) rather than opening a public issue. Please do not disclose it publicly until a fix is available.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and which version or commit you tested. You will get a reply from a human.

This information is also published in [`/.well-known/security.txt`](/.well-known/security.txt).
