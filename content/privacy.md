This website is static. It sets no cookies, runs no scripts, loads nothing from third parties, and has no accounts, forms, or analytics.

It is served by Cloudflare, which processes the connection data needed to deliver a page, such as your IP address, under [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). We do not receive or keep that data.

If you email [hello@varyk.com](mailto:hello@varyk.com) or [security@varyk.com](mailto:security@varyk.com), we keep the message and your address for as long as the correspondence needs, and use them for nothing else.
