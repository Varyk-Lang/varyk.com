## Source and issues

Varyk is developed in the open at [github.com/Varyk-Lang/varyk](https://github.com/Varyk-Lang/varyk). Discussion happens in the [issues](https://github.com/Varyk-Lang/varyk/issues): bug reports, questions about the design, and proposals all go there. Until milestone 1 lands, the design itself is the thing to comment on.

## Contact

- General: [hello@varyk.com](mailto:hello@varyk.com)
- Security reports: [security@varyk.com](mailto:security@varyk.com). See the [security page](/security/) before reporting.

## News

New releases and design changes are announced on the [blog](/blog/), which has an [Atom feed](/blog/atom.xml).
